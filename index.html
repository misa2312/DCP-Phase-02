Standing rule

Applies to every vendor and every purchase value.

All vendor communication runs through Alicia — quotes, NDAs, scope questions and GTCs. The team does not approach vendors directly, regardless of purchase value. Amended 22 July 2026 — supersedes the earlier $25k threshold, under which the team could transact directly with registered vendors below that value.

Three routes to a report

Which route applies depends on whether the vendor is already onboarded in the ABB system.

Direct onboarding

Standard route · in use
  1. Vendor returns four signed documents: GTCs, vendor template, bank template, party ID PDF
  2. Alicia hands the complete set to Agnieszka
  3. Onboarding runs in the system ~3 weeks
  4. Purchase requisition and PO can be raised

Watch: the three weeks only start once all four documents are in. A vendor who does not return them cannot be onboarded at all.

Reseller

Fast route · feasibility open
  1. Buy through a supplier already onboarded at ABB
  2. That supplier purchases the report from the publisher and resells it
  3. No new onboarding — avoids the three-week wait

Candidates: Alicia's software reseller (willing, subject to the publisher agreeing) and Cesmay, a French supplier with precedent — a Mordor Intelligence report roughly two years ago.

Unanswered: reseller margin, and how the enterprise licence and Eraneos access work contractually through a third party.

P-card

Bypass · likely closed
  1. Pay by company card — no onboarding and no purchase order

Blocked: sits outside the standard process, so Alicia cannot support it, and it may be US-only. Simona covers ABB Italy while our budget sits in the Swiss reporting unit, and Stephen has since been ruled out as the Swiss contact.
