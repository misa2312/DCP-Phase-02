Three routes to a report

Which route applies depends on whether the vendor is already onboarded in the ABB system.

Direct onboarding

Standard route · in use
  1. Vendor returns four signed documents: GTCs, vendor template, bank template, party ID PDF
  2. Alicia hands the complete set to Agnieszka Partyka
  3. Onboarding runs in the system ~3 weeks
  4. Purchase requisition and PO can be raised

Watch: the three weeks only start once all four documents are in. A vendor who does not return them cannot be onboarded at all.

Reseller

Fast route · available now
  1. Buy through a supplier already onboarded at ABB
  2. That supplier purchases the report from the publisher and resells it
  3. No new onboarding — avoids the three-week wait

Candidates: SoftwareOne and Candex — both onboarded resellers that buy directly from the publisher, now for a ~5% surcharge (up from 3%; exact figure to confirm). Candex is already set up for the Swiss reporting unit. Research and Markets — a reports reseller (650+ suppliers, specific coverage not listed) that carries no surcharge, but is not yet onboarded (3–6 weeks).

Preference: given the surcharge, the team leans toward a direct PR via Agnieszka Baginska where possible, using a reseller only as fallback if the direct route stalls. The team would need support from Alicia or Agnieszka to operate a reseller.

Candex how-to: business provides the Candex link to Agnieszka Baginska, who follows the instructions. One PO per supplier; overall Candex use is capped at ~€30k/year and no more than 3 POs/year.

For GVR / GMI: not available in CHAMS, so a reseller is the fallback if a direct PR cannot be raised — otherwise the direct PR route is preferred to avoid the surcharge.

P-card

Fast route · process shared
  1. Pay by company card — no onboarding and no purchase order

Status: the P-card process has been shared with Francesca's team. Sits outside the standard onboarding route and may not apply to every region.

Risk — vendor credibility

Identified 29 July; position updated 5 August.

Open risk

Two Reddit threads flagged several scouted vendors as unreliable — alleging advertised reports don't always exist and estimates are assembled from quick web searches rather than rigorous research. Why it matters: LSOs and future dashboard users may run the same search and surface the same threads, undermining confidence in the published figures. Credibility was agreed as the paramount criterion, given the intent for wider adoption.

Current position (5 Aug)

Credibility is treated as an explicit selection dimension, mapped alongside scope fit, forecast horizon and geographic granularity — not pass/fail. The trade-off is accepted transparently: GVR / GMI give the strongest scope match, granularity and longest horizon (2035) but carry higher credibility risk; Frost & Sullivan, Beroe, ResearchWire are more credible and sit outside the flagged cluster. A mixed-vendor approach — pairing a high-scope-fit but riskier vendor with a more credible one — is under consideration. On methodology: where primary research would significantly extend delivery of a custom report, relying on secondary sources is considered sufficient for Phase 2; primary-source customisation is noted as a scale-up recommendation for later.

How reliable is the flag itself?

  • The Reddit threads carry no evidential weight — unverified crowd sentiment, not a finding. Phase 1 vendors (e.g. Straits Research) appear on the same list, and part of the criticism simply rests on vendors being India-based, which is not a basis for a quality judgement. Recorded for completeness only; it moved no assessment materially.
  • A structured methodology and document cross-check was run instead (Alma), assessing each vendor on disclosure, primary-research evidence, internal/arithmetic consistency, document QC, and firm standing. This — not the Reddit sentiment — is the basis for the flags.
  • AI usage is not itself a red flag — what matters is where in the process it is applied.
One hard external finding: Mordor Intelligence holds a 51% stake in Global Market Insights (since Sept 2023), undisclosed by either vendor — and GMI's proposal names Mordor as an inferior competitor. This verifiable, undisclosed conflict directly affects how both bids should be read.

Validation options

  • ABB business references: ask the colleagues who originally requested a report whether the figures matched expectations. Alicia asked GVR / GMI / FBI for prior ABB contacts — GMI and FBI provided them, GVR declined. Tracked as a separate "internal-user feedback" dimension.
  • National association benchmarks: compare country figures against consolidated national data (e.g. Italy, already used in Stratos). Some products / countries only, post-purchase.
  • Timing constraint: pre-purchase validation is only possible for off-the-shelf reports. For customised reports, country estimates appear only after purchase — so validation is largely post-purchase, which is the risk to manage up front.
